Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Linwell is an app that lets family members share their location with each other. This policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and how you can control or delete it.

1. Who we are

Linwell is an independently developed app by Linwell. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@getlinwell.com or visit getlinwell.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: your name, email and password (the password is stored encrypted; we can never see it).
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one.
  • Precise location, including when the app is closed or not in use, if you turn on “Always share”.
  • Speed and direction of travel, calculated from GPS.
  • Route history (location points while you move), to show trips.
  • Family chat messages, photos you send to the chat and locations you share in a message.
  • Phone setup status (whether location, precise location, notifications, unrestricted battery and lock screen notifications are on), so your family knows if they can see you properly.
  • Your phone's battery level.
  • Places your family creates (for example “Home”) and arrivals and departures at those places.
  • Speed alerts, when someone goes over the limit set by the admin.
  • SOS alerts you send, with your location at that moment.
  • Low-battery and weather alerts sent to your family.
  • Your family: its name, who is an admin and who can see whom.
  • Whether you paused location sharing and until when.
  • Lost mode: if an admin marks your phone as lost, the message and contact phone number they write, who turned it on, and when it was turned on and off.
  • A device identifier and the app language, used to send you notifications in your language.

3. Why we use background location

Linwell collects your location even when the app is closed so your family can see you on the map and receive arrival and departure notices, speed alerts and SOS alerts. While this happens you will see a persistent notification. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → “Always share” or in Android's permissions.

4. How we use your data

  • To show your location only to family members the admin has allowed.
  • To send arrival, departure, speed, low-battery, weather and SOS notifications.
  • To let your family admin ring your phone with an alarm when they urgently need to reach you (it rings even on silent).
  • Lost mode: to show the message and contact phone number written by the admin on the lost phone's lock screen, so whoever finds it can return it. Anyone holding the phone can read them without unlocking it.
  • To let you sign in and reset your password.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles and we never sell your data.

5. Who can see your data

  • Your family's admin can see your location and driving details.
  • Your family's admin can also see a driving summary for each member: miles, trips, top speed and speeding.
  • Other members can see you only if the admin allows it, and see speed and routes only with that permission.
  • These rules are enforced on our server, not just in the app.

6. Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts the database and accounts.
  • Google Maps: displays the map and searches addresses.
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver notifications.
  • Brevo: sends password reset emails.
  • Open-Meteo: receives an approximate location (rounded to about 100 m) to look up the weather. It does not receive your name or email.
  • These providers process data only to provide their service.

7. How long we keep data

  • Routes are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Place arrivals and departures and speed alerts are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Chat messages are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Chat photos can only be viewed for 24 hours. After that, they are deleted from the server the next time the sender opens the chat.
  • Weather alerts are kept for up to 7 days and then deleted automatically.
  • SOS and low-battery alerts are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Your latest location, profile and places are kept while your account exists.
  • Each Lost mode activation (message and contact phone number) is kept while it is active and deleted automatically once it has been turned off and 90 days have passed since it was turned on.
  • When you delete your account, we permanently delete your data.

8. How to delete your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your profile, photo, location, history, messages and notification settings. If you don't have the app installed, email hello@getlinwell.com with the subject “Delete my account” from your account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and database access is protected by rules that prevent anyone from seeing data that isn't theirs.

10. Minimum age

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Linwell and join a family.
  • You must be at least 18 years old to create a family and be its admin. The admin is responsible for inviting only people who agree to share their location.
  • Linwell is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and let you know in the app if the change is significant.
