Delete your Linwell account

You can delete your Linwell account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

From the app

  1. Open Linwell and sign in.
  2. Go to the Family tab (Admin if you are an admin) and tap your photo at the top right to open My settings.
  3. At the bottom of My settings, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.

If you're your family's only admin, your family will also be deleted for all its members. To keep it, appoint another admin first. If there is another admin, only your account is deleted and the family stays.

If you no longer have the app

Email hello@getlinwell.com with the subject “Delete my account” from your account's email address. We will delete it within 30 days.

Email us

What is deleted

  • Your profile and name
  • Your profile photo
  • Your location
  • Your route history
  • Your chat messages
  • Your notification settings

When

  • From the app: your data is permanently deleted when you confirm. This cannot be undone.
  • By email: within 30 days of your request.
