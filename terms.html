Terms of Use

Last updated: November 25, 2026

By creating a Linwell account you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy.

1. What Linwell is

Linwell lets family members see each other on a map, get arrival and departure notices, speed and SOS alerts, and talk in a family chat.

2. Minimum age

  • You must be at least 13 years old to create an account and join a family.
  • You must be at least 18 years old to create a family and be its admin.

3. Consent

  • Only invite people who know they will share their location and agree to it.
  • Using Linwell to track someone without their knowledge, or to harass or control another person, is not allowed.
  • Every member can pause or stop sharing their location, leave the family or delete their account at any time.

4. Not an emergency service

Linwell depends on the phone's GPS, battery and connection, so locations may be unavailable or inaccurate. The SOS button alerts your family, not emergency services: in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

5. Your account

You are responsible for what happens with your account. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.

6. Pet and thing QR tags

  • You may only create tags for your own pets and things or your family's.
  • Don't put other people's details on a tag or in its details without their permission.
  • Anyone sending a message from a tag must not send false or offensive information or advertising. We may block abusive messages.
  • Linwell helps people reach you, but it can't guarantee that a lost pet or thing will be found.
